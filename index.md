<div class="callout">
  <p><strong>Summary.</strong> FilaTare is offline first. Your spool inventory, projects, print logs and scale readings are stored on your device. We do not run advertising, we do not sell data, and we do not build user profiles.</p>

<h2>What is stored on your device</h2>
<p>Spool records (brand, material, colour, part number, price, purchase date, weights, barcode, storage location), projects and parts, print logs, scale readings, tags, alert history and your app preferences.</p>

<h2>Account information</h2>
<p>If you sign in with Google we receive your email address and display name from that provider so the app can show who is signed in and label your backups. We do not receive your password. Signing in is optional — the app works fully offline as a guest.</p>

<h2>Backups you create</h2>
<p>Choosing <em>Back up now</em> writes a single JSON snapshot of your workshop to the location you pick in your own cloud storage. That file is under your control and is governed by your storage provider's terms. We do not hold a copy.</p>

<h2>Camera</h2>
<p>The camera is used only while the scanner screen is open, to read a barcode or QR code. Frames are processed on your device by Google ML Kit and are never uploaded or saved by FilaTare.</p>

<h2>Notifications</h2>
<p>Low stock alerts are generated on your device from your own thresholds. Enabling them requires the notification permission and does not send any data off the device.</p>

<h2>Analytics and tracking</h2>
<p>FilaTare contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics tracking. Google Play Services components used for sign-in and barcode scanning are governed by <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" rel="noopener">Google's privacy policy</a>.</p>

<h2>Data retention and deletion</h2>
<p>Data stays on your device until you remove it. Deleting a spool removes it and its readings. <em>Delete account</em> in Settings erases all local data immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored on the device.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>FilaTare is intended for hobbyist and professional makers and is not directed at children under 13.</p>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>Because your data is local, you can export it at any time with <em>Back up now</em>, and erase it at any time with <em>Delete account</em>. For any privacy question contact <a href="mailto:wild.sulfur93@eagereverest.com">wild.sulfur93@eagereverest.com</a>.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy as the app develops. The version shown inside your installed release is the one that applies to you, and the date at the top of this page reflects the latest published revision.</p>
